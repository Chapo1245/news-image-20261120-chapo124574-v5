Entry for the [news-image-20261120 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120): sum up 20 November 2026's world news in one image.

## The page

One front page, 1024×1024, 36 stories from Friday 20 November 2026 — three large leads (Cyclone Akvan's landfall at Gopalpur, the Russian strikes on Kyiv's bridges, Anthropic's $97.17 IPO debut), ten illustrated cards, and 23 numbered briefs, across seven regions. Every story carries its source. A dotted world map pins the 13 illustrated stories, and a DATE CHECK box lists the look-alike stories that are *not* 20 November news and were deliberately left out.

## How it is built

`entry.png` is pre-rendered with Pillow (original flat illustrations, no stock photos, no copied layouts). `main.py` only copies it to the output directory, so runs are byte-identical.

## Sources

Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, The Hindu, IMD, UNEP, IAEA, UNICEF, Sensor Tower, Al Jazeera, Google. Disputed claims are attributed, not stated as fact.
